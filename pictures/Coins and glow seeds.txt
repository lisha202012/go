OVERVIEW

The GOFAM reward system is built around two complementary concepts:

?? FLOW

Grow Yourself

?? GLOW

Help Others Grow

Users earn GLOW Seeds by growing themselves, then plant those Seeds in others.

Every accepted GLOW Seed creates a shared growth experience between two people.

The app should consistently reinforce:

Master the Flow. Share the Glow.

PART 2 — GOFAM GLOW SEEDS

?

Core Philosophy

A GLOW Seed represents one person believing in another person’s growth.

It is not a referral reward.

It is not a gift.

It is not a coupon.

It is an act of encouragement.

?

Every GLOW Seed is identical.

Every GLOW Seed is mysterious.

Nobody knows what Virtue it contains.

Not the sender.

Not the receiver.

Only when the Seed blooms is its Virtue revealed.

?

Earning GLOW Seeds

GLOW Seeds are earned only through personal growth.

Typical source:

Complete a FLOW Week

?

Earn:

10 GLOW Seeds

(7 Hill Rewards + 3 Completion Bonus)

Users cannot purchase GLOW Seeds.

Users cannot trade GLOW Seeds.

Users cannot gift unused Seeds to others.

?

Maximum Inventory

A user may hold a maximum of:

49 GLOW Seeds

Why?

49 (7×7) represents mastery within GOFAM.

If a user already has 49 Seeds,

newly earned Seeds are forfeited.

This encourages generosity instead of hoarding.

The inventory should display:

37 / 49 GLOW Seeds

?

Sending Rules

A user may send

One GLOW Seed

to the

same Friend

per calendar month.

Example:

Second Seed to Maya in July

? Not Allowed

August

? Allowed again

?

Receiving Rules

There is no limit on how many GLOW Seeds a user may receive.

Each accepted Seed has the opportunity to activate a different Virtue.

Duplicate Virtues do not stack.

?

Social Connections

GOFAM is not a messaging platform.

Users connect using:

* Username
* QR Code
* Follow Requests

Only mutual Friends may exchange GLOW Seeds.

This protects privacy and reduces spam.

Privacy

Visible:

* Avatar
* Tree
* Hills
* Achievements

* Phone number

* Personal information
* Location

No private messaging exists within GOFAM.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES

The final implementation should ensure:

* FLOW always rewards personal effort.
* GLOW always rewards encouragement.
* GLOW Seeds are scarce enough to feel valuable.
* Every accepted Seed creates surprise through a hidden Bloom.
* Every Bloom creates a shared monthly journey between two Friends.
* Users must choose carefully who to encourage, because Seeds are earned through effort, limited in inventory (49), and disappear if never accepted.
* The platform remains safe through mutual friendships, usernames, and the absence of private messaging.

?

GOFAM Philosophy

Every Family Is a Garden.
Plant a Seed. Grow Greatness.

A user’s Tree reflects how they grow.

A user’s Forest reflects how many people they have encouraged to grow.

Together, FLOW and GLOW create an ecosystem where personal growth and community encouragement reinforce each other, making growth meaningful, social, and sustainable.


Want to clarify the logic for Bloom Seeds and Virtues.

Every time a member (user) completes 3 Missions in a particular Hill, they receive a Glow seed.

This Glow seed can be used to initiate a new member (becomes friend by default) or encourage a friend (through request and accept request).

When a member gets a glow seeds from another member, they can choose to accept it. Then, the Glow seed blooms into a virtue. The sender gets an intimation that the seed they planted has bloomed. Both of them would simultaneously receive a virtue that activates/boosts their Hill for double coins that entire calendar month (if the Hill has already been boosted, then it won’t count).

A member can send a maximum of 1 Glow seed to a user in a month, and can receive unlimited Glow seeds which can be bloomed or faded away at the end of the month. 

A member can accumulate Glow seeds up to 49 at any given time. And can only earn the Glow seeds through Flow (meaning completing all 3 missions for the Hill).

Guiding Principle for Development

The system should always reinforce a simple and memorable cycle:

FLOW earns GLOW. GLOW strengthens FLOW.

Users first invest in their own growth to earn GLOW Seeds. They then share those Seeds with friends, creating shared Virtues that motivate both people to complete more Missions together. This positive feedback loop is the heart of the GOFAM ecosystem and should be reflected consistently in the app’s UX, animations, notifications, and reward logic.
